Information
Science

The study of how people, organizations, and technology work with information. Where computer science focuses on how computing systems are built, information science focuses on how people use them, from data and design to security and networks.

A Brief History

The Official Association for Students in Information Science (OASIS) was founded in 2020. Our founders saw a need for more professional and peer support for Information Science students.

Today we continue to provide that support through our various events and initiatives while welcoming anyone regardless of year or major.
